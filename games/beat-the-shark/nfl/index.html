About Beat the Shark: NFL

Before a ball is snapped, FixtureShark's model simulates the season thousands of times and predicts how many games your team wins. Your job is to win more: as head coach, calling the game plan, the fourth downs and the half-time adjustments, or as general manager, running the cap and the trades. Every team and player is invented; the home advantage and the shape of the scores come from FixtureShark's real NFL models.

  • Fixtures & Results — the Elo model beside the betting line, and how each game might finish
  • League Table — the standings with the official tie-breaks
  • Snap Outlook — who plays, and how much
