About the poker trainers

Beat the Shark's poker trainers teach Texas hold'em one idea at a time, starting with reading hands: your best five cards from seven, and which of two hands wins. Every deal is chosen to teach, including the hands people misread. No money or chips are involved. Coming next: outs and draws, pot odds, equity, then pre-flop and post-flop decisions.
