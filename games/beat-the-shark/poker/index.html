About the poker trainers

Beat the Shark's poker trainers teach Texas hold'em one idea at a time, starting with reading hands (your best five cards from seven, and which of two hands wins) and counting outs (the cards that make your hand, and the chance of getting one). Every deal is chosen to teach. No money or chips are involved. Then pot odds: whether the price is right to call; equity: both hands face up, how often each wins; and pre-flop: heads-up shove or fold against the equilibrium. Coming next: ranges and post-flop decisions.
