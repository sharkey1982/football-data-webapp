About Beat the Shark

Before a ball is kicked, FixtureShark's model simulates the season thousands of times and predicts where your club will finish. Your job is to beat that prediction — as manager, owner or star player. Every club, player and pundit is fictional; the model behind it is the real one.

The ideas in the game are the site's real tools:

  • Team Strength — every club's attack and defence, as expected goals
  • Starting Lineups — the XI the model expects each club to field
  • Fixture Heat Map — who has the kindest run of fixtures
  • Model Accuracy — how often the predictions come true
