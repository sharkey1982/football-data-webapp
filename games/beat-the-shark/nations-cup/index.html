About Beat the Shark: Nations Cup

A mixed national team tennis event across four surfaces: hard, clay, grass and indoor hard. Pick a real nation, rated by FixtureShark's tennis Elo, then three men and three women from twelve invented players. Each tie is five rubbers (men's and women's singles, men's, women's and mixed doubles), so nobody plays only what they are best at. Weigh surfaces, left-handers and partnerships, keep the money out of the red, and win the Nations Cup.

  • Head to head — any two players' chances, by surface
  • Your Player — every player's record and surface splits
  • Tournaments — every event, draw by draw
