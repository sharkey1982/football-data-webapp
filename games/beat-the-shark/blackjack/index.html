About the Blackjack trainer

A blackjack trainer, not a gambling game. You play with Shark Chips, which are fictional: there are no deposits, no purchases, no prizes, and chips can't be cashed or swapped for anything. Every decision you make is checked against basic strategy for the table's rules, with what it costs on average when it isn't the best play, and kept separate from whether the hand won. A good result can follow a bad decision, and a good decision can lose: over many hands, the decisions are what count.
