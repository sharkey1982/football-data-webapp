About Beat the Shark: World Cup

A five-a-side World Cup. You manage a real nation, rated by FixtureShark's international Elo, with invented players: pick seven from ten, choose the shape for each match, deal with injuries, suspensions and tired legs, keep the money out of the red, and win the World Cup. Goals come from FixtureShark's international model on the Elo gap. Every player is invented.

  • Your Team — any nation's real squad, record and rating
  • Tournaments — every World Cup, bracket by bracket
  • Through Time — the world's top ten since 1872
